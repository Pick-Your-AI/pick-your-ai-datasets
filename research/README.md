# Research notes

Research notes are Markdown, organized by work package, for example `research/sources/` (DATA-02) and `research/benchmarks/` (DATA-07). Findings that are not yet complete stay here until they meet the record schema and publication gates. Cite upstream research by repository, path and commit, and do not restate it. The [DATA-01.05 existing research ownership audit](EXISTING_RESEARCH.md) is a **proposed, version-pinned handoff**, not an accepted research or publication decision.
